CocoTrade – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CocoTrade collects, uses, and shares your personal information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: We use Google Sign-In to authenticate you. When you sign in, we receive your basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture URL.
  • User-Generated Content: We collect the videos you upload to our platform, along with any metadata you provide, such as captions, location, and contact information.
  • Device Information: We may collect basic information about your device to improve the app's performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service.
  • To authenticate your identity and link your uploaded content to your account.
  • To allow other users to see the content you share publicly and contact you if you provide contact information.

3. Sharing of Information

Any content you upload to CocoTrade, including your name, location, and contact information if provided, is shared publicly and can be viewed by all users of the application. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage

We use Firebase (a Google service) for user authentication and database management. Videos are hosted using Cloudinary. These third-party services have their own privacy policies.

5. Content Deletion

Users can delete their own uploaded videos. If you wish to delete your account or have your data removed, please contact us at cocotrade262@gmail.com.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at cocotrade262@gmail.com.
